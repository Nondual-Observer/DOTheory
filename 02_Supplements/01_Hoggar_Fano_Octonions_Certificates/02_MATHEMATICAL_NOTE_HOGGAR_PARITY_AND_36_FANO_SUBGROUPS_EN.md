## 2. Proposition A (Sign-Table Parity and Closed Formula for $K_n$)

**Exact phase convention.** Let $d = 2^n$ and $a = (a_x, a_z) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$. We fix the **tensor Hermitian convention** with integer phase exponent:
$$P_a = i^{f(a_x, a_z)} X^{a_x} Z^{a_z}, \qquad f(a_x, a_z) = \sum_{j=1}^n a_{x,j} a_{z,j} \in \mathbb{Z}$$
(where $a_{x,j}, a_{z,j} \in \{0,1\}$ are summed in $\mathbb{Z}$ and $i^f$ is taken modulo $4$), whereas the symplectic pairing $\omega(a,b) = a_x \cdot b_z + a_z \cdot b_x \pmod 2$ is evaluated over $\mathbb{F}_2$. This distinction is essential: reducing the inner product $a_x \cdot a_z$ modulo $2$ first flips the signs of 10 operators (at $n = 3$) and produces a different Hermitian section with $K_3 = 336$ instead of $270$.

For a pure normalized state $\psi \in \mathbb{C}^8$, set $E(a) = \langle\psi | P_a | \psi\rangle$, $s(0) = 0$, and $s(a) = 1$ when $E(a) < 0$ ($s(a) = 0$ otherwise) for $a \neq 0$. For commuting $P_a, P_b$, define $\varepsilon(a,b) \in \{0,1\}$ by $P_a P_b = (-1)^{\varepsilon(a,b)} P_{a+b}$.

**Proposition A.** Let $\psi \in \mathbb{C}^8$ be any Pauli-covariant SIC fiducial ($|E(a)|^2 = 1/9$ for all $a \neq 0$). Then for any choice of Hermitian representatives $P_a$, the following invariant congruence holds:
$$e := \sum_{a \neq 0} s(a) \equiv 1 + K \pmod 2, \qquad K = \#\bigl\{\{a,b\} : a \neq b,\; a,b \neq 0,\; [P_a, P_b] = 0,\; \varepsilon(a,b) = 1\bigr\}.$$
In the tensor Hermitian convention above, $K = K_3 = 270$, whence $e \equiv 1 \pmod 2$ (the number of negative expectation values is odd).

**Proof.** Comparing the coefficients of $P_c$ ($c \neq 0$) in the purity identity $\rho^2 = \rho$ gives $\sum_p t_p = 9\,\operatorname{sgn} E(c)$ over the $15$ unordered commuting pairs $\{a, a+c\} \subset c^\perp \setminus \{0, c\}$, where $t_p = \operatorname{sgn} E(a)\operatorname{sgn} E(a+c)(-1)^{\varepsilon(a, a+c)} \in \{\pm 1\}$. Consequently, the number of pairs with $t_p = -1$ is either $3$ (when $E(c) > 0$) or $12$ (when $E(c) < 0$), which in both cases is congruent to $1 + s(c) \pmod 2$. Summing over all $63$ nonzero $c$, we observe that each $s(a)$ enters as the sign of one of the two factors exactly $30$ times, while each unordered commuting pair $\{a,b\}$ enters exactly once (for $c = a + b$). We obtain $K \equiv 63 + e \equiv 1 + e \pmod 2$. $\blacksquare$

**Remarks.**
1. **What depends on the section vs. what is invariant.** The quantity $K$ is a constant of the chosen Hermitian representatives $P_a$, not of the abstract Pauli group. Under an admissible sign change $P'_a = (-1)^{r(a)} P_a$ ($r(0) = 0$), the sign table and cocycle transform as $s' = s + r$ and $\varepsilon'(a,b) = \varepsilon(a,b) + r(a) + r(b) + r(a+b) \pmod 2$. In the sum $K$, each $r(a)$ is counted $30 + 15 = 45$ (an odd number!) times, so $K' \equiv K + \sum_{a \neq 0} r(a) \pmod 2$ and $e' \equiv e + \sum_{a \neq 0} r(a) \pmod 2$: the congruence $e' \equiv 1 + K' \pmod 2$ is invariant, whereas the literal oddness $e = 1$ pertains to the fixed section (for instance, flipping the sign of a single operator $P_{X_1}$ gives $K: 270 \to 315$ and $e: 1 \to 0$).
2. **Analytical formula for $K_n$ in the tensor convention.** The phase of the product $P_a P_b$ factors across qubits: on a single qubit, an ordered pair of operators $(p, q)$ contributes a factor $\pm i$ (with sign given by the Levi-Civita symbol $\epsilon_{pqr}$) if $p \neq q$ and both are non-identity ($6$ pairs), and $1$ otherwise ($10$ pairs). Operators $P_a$ and $P_b$ commute if and only if the number of anticommuting positions is even ($2k \ge 0$); for $2k \ge 2$ the product of phases is $(-1)^k \prod \epsilon$, and exactly half of the $6^{2k}$ sign patterns yield $-1$. Dividing the number of ordered pairs by $2$, we obtain:
   $$K_n = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{k \ge 1} \binom{n}{2k} 6^{2k} 10^{\,n-2k} = \frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{16^n + 4^n}{2} - 10^n\right).$$
   Hence $K_1 = 0$, $K_2 = 9$, **$K_3 = 270$**, $K_4 = 5724$, $K_5 = 106\,956$, $K_6 = 1\,956\,360$—verified by direct enumeration for $n = 1\dots 6$ and exact Gaussian integers ([`04_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ФАЗ_И_ФОРМУЛ.json`](./certificates_and_julia/audit_111/04_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ФАЗ_И_ФОРМУЛ.json)).
3. **Invariance of the top Zhegalkin term under the Clifford group.** Set $s(0) = 0$. Then the degree-$6$ coefficient of the Zhegalkin polynomial of $s\colon \mathbb{F}_2^6 \to \mathbb{F}_2$ equals $\sum_{a \in \mathbb{F}_2^6} s(a) \equiv e \pmod 2$. Under conjugation by a Clifford unitary operator, the labels $a \in \mathbb{F}_2^6$ are bijectively permuted (preserving the sum over all $64$ points), and the sign shift $r(a)$ of each generating gate `H`, `S`, and `CNOT` has even Hamming weight on $\mathbb{F}_2^6$. Therefore, the entire Clifford class in the tensor convention preserves $e \equiv 1 \pmod 2$.

## 3. Proposition B (Zorn's Octonions, 36 Fano Subgroups, and Real Structures)

**Exact groups and descent of the polar form.**
- The full projective group of Clifford symmetries of the entire 64-line Hoggar SIC has order $64 \times 6048 = 387\,072$ (unitary) and $64 \times 12\,096 = 774\,144$ (with antiunitary operators). Below, $U \subset \mathrm{Sp}(6,2)$ (`|U| = 6048`, $U \cong \mathrm{PSU}_3(3)$) denotes the symplectic image of the unitary **stabilizer of a single fiducial** (equivalently, the quotient of the full unitary SIC symmetry group by the 64 Pauli translations), and $G \subset \mathrm{Sp}(6,2)$ (`|G| = 12\,096`, $G \cong G_2(2) \cong \mathrm{PSU}_3(3) : 2$) denotes the extended symplectic image including antiunitary symmetries.
- In Zorn's vector-matrix octonion algebra $\mathrm{Oct}(\mathbb{F}_2)$ on $(a, v; w, b) \in \mathbb{F}_2 \oplus W \oplus W^* \oplus \mathbb{F}_2$ ($W = \mathbb{F}_2^3$), the norm $N(a,v;w,b) = ab + v\cdot w$ on the trace-zero subspace $T_0 = \{a = b\}$ **does not descend** to the quotient $V = T_0 / \mathbb{F}_2\cdot 1$, since $0$ and $1 = (1,0;0,1)$ lie in the same coset while $N(0) = 0 \neq N(1) = 1$. What descends well-definedly to $V \cong W \oplus W^*$ is the **polar bilinear form** of the norm, coinciding with $\omega$; the quadratic form $Q_0(v,w) = v\cdot w$ arises after choosing the section $(0, v; w, 0) \subset T_0$.
- Without relying on any literature group order, it is proved exhaustively ([`03_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ГРУПП_И_ЦОРНА.json`](./certificates_and_julia/audit_111/03_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ГРУПП_И_ЦОРНА.json)) that every automorphism of $\mathrm{Oct}(\mathbb{F}_2)$ maps the standard idempotent $e = (1,0;0,0)$ to one of the `72` trace-$1$ idempotents, and maps an ordered basis of the three-dimensional Peirce subspace $U_e = \{u : eu = u,\; ue = 0\}$ to one of its $7 \cdot 6 \cdot 4 = 168$ bases (the remaining images being uniquely determined by the products $u_2 u_3, u_3 u_1, u_1 u_2$, and $1 + e$). All $72 \times 168 = 12\,096$ candidates are algebra automorphisms, their induced action on $V$ literally coincides with the group $G$ (`6048` unitary + `6048` antiunitary Hoggar symmetries), and closure and homomorphism of the 8-column lift are verified across all $12\,096^2 = 146\,313\,216$ ordered pairs.

**Proposition B.**
1. In the full symplectic space $(\mathbb{F}_2^6, \omega)$ there are `135` Lagrangian subspaces and `4320` unordered pairs of complementary Lagrangian subspaces $\{L_1, L_2\}$ (`120` pairs for each of the `36` plus-type quadratic forms). Among them, exactly **36 pairs** have a common stabilizer of order `168` in the unitary stabilizer $U$ (acting on $L_1$ as the full Fano group $\mathrm{GL}_3(2)$); the corresponding `36` Levi subgroups in $U$ are pairwise distinct.
2. The map $\{L_1, L_2\} \mapsto Q(x_1 + x_2) = \omega(x_1, x_2)$ ($x_i \in L_i$) defines a bijection from these **36 distinguished pairs** (and their stabilizers $\mathrm{GL}_3(2) \subset U$) onto all **36 quadratic forms of plus-type** polarizing to $\omega$. The group $U$ acts transitively on them (rank `4`, suborbits `1, 7, 7, 21`): for the standard form $Q_0(a) = a_x \cdot a_z \pmod 2$ we have $\mathrm{Stab}_U(Q_0) \cong \mathrm{GL}_3(2)$ of order **168**, whereas in the extended group $G$ of order `12 096` the stabilizer $\mathrm{Stab}_G(Q_0)$ has order **336** (on the `28` minus-type forms, the stabilizer in $U$ has order `216`).
3. **Description via Zorn idempotents ([`09_ФОРМЫ_И_ИДЕМПОТЕНТЫ.jl`](./certificates_and_julia/audit_111/09_ФОРМЫ_И_ИДЕМПОТЕНТЫ.jl)).** The `72` trace-$1$ idempotents in $\mathrm{Oct}(\mathbb{F}_2)$ partition into `36` complementary pairs $\{e, 1+e\}$ (under $U$, the idempotents themselves form two orbits of size `36`). Restricting the norm $N$ to the Peirce section $U_e \oplus V_e$ defines a bijection between the `36` pairs $\{e, 1+e\}$ and the `36` plus-type quadratic forms.
4. **Pauli operator phases and the discrete family of real structures ([`16_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ВЕЩЕСТВЕННЫХ_СТРУКТУР.json`](./certificates_and_julia/audit_111/16_СЕРТИФИКАТ_ВЕЩЕСТВЕННЫХ_СТРУКТУР.json)).** In the Hermitian basis $P_a$, the form $Q_0(a) = a_x \cdot a_z \pmod 2$ partitions the $64$ operators into `36` real symmetric ($Q_0(a) = 0$) and `28` **purely imaginary** skew-symmetric ($Q_0(a) = 1$) matrices; a real skew-symmetric basis of the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ is formed by the matrices $-i P_a$ with $Q_0(a) = 1$ (or the real products $X^{a_x} Z^{a_z}$). If for coordinatewise complex conjugation $K_0$ we consider the discrete family of antiunitary involutions $J_c = P_c K_0$ ($c \in \mathbb{F}_2^6$, up to phase), then
   $$J_c^2 = (-1)^{Q_0(c)} I, \qquad J_c P_a J_c^{-1} = (-1)^{Q_0(a) + \omega(c,a)} P_a.$$
   Within this 64-element family of Pauli conjugations there are exactly **36 real structures** ($J_c^2 = I$) and **28 quaternionic structures** ($J_c^2 = -I$).
