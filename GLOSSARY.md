# DOT Glossary of Terms and Conceptual Concordance
## Теория Наблюдаемого Различения (ТНР) — Англо-русский терминологический словарь

This glossary fixes the English translation for foundational concepts, mathematical terms, domain families, relation types, and epistemic status markers used across Volume I of **Distinction and Observability Theory (DOT)**.

---

### 1. Foundational Epistemic & Ontological Concepts / Основные онтологические понятия

| Russian Term | English Term | Meaning / Formal Context in DOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Теория Наблюдаемого Различения (ТНР)** | **Distinction and Observability Theory (DOT)** | Research program investigating how mathematical structures are presented across distinct representations without losing essential work. |
| **Различение** | **Distinction** | The primary primitive operation; identifying difference or partition across states. |
| **Положение «нет ничего, кроме различения»** | **The premise "there is nothing except distinction"** | The foundational ontological stance of DOT ([Prem] / [Исх]). |
| **Акт различения** | **Act of distinction** | The explicit instantiation of drawing a distinction using available language. |
| **Сохранное различение** | **Preserved distinction** | A distinction that remains discriminable after projection or change of representation. |
| **Сохранное продолжение** | **Preserved continuation** | The ability to proceed with subsequent actions/inquiries without loss of required data. |
| **Защищаемая работа** | **Protected work** | The specific operations, relations, or queries that a representation must preserve. |
| **Положение описания** | **Stance of description / Description setting** | The explicit perspective, available primitives, and constraints from which a model is formulated. |
| **Рамка ($\Gamma$)** | **Frame ($\Gamma$)** | The formal envelope specifying admissible representations, relations, and operations. |
| **Внешняя рамка** | **External frame** | The meta-level describing tasks, models, and transitions. |
| **Внутренняя рамка** | **Internal frame** | The local level describing states and transitions within a specific model. |
| **Охватывающая рамка** | **Enclosing frame / Encompassing frame** | A higher-level frame embedding multiple lower-level descriptions. |
| **Целое** | **Whole / Unified whole** | A bounded configuration of participants, relations, and admissible transformations. |
| **Сопряжённое целое** | **Conjugate whole** | A paired structure exhibiting dual or symmetric counterparts under an involution. |
| **Связанное целое** | **Bound whole / Connected whole** | An integrated structure whose parts cannot be decomposed without severance of relations. |
| **Раскрываемое целое** | **Unfoldable whole / Disclosable whole** | A composite entity possessing an explicit internal structure that can be unraveled. |
| **Вычислительное целое** | **Computational whole** | The complete apparatus of states, cost accounting, actions, memory, and continuation. |
| **Структурная единица** | **Structural unit** | A single presented composite whole equipped with its rule of disclosure. |
| **Раскрытие** | **Disclosure / Unfolding** | The formal exhibition of internal states, witnesses, or subcomponents. |
| **Обратная сборка** | **Inverse assembly / Reassembly** | Recovering the source whole from partial presentations using auxiliary fulcrums. |
| **Разрез** | **Cut / Partition** | Partitioning a whole into accessible and latent components. |
| **Носитель** | **Carrier / Carrier set** | The underlying set or space on which states, questions, or relations are defined. |
| **Подноситель** | **Subcarrier** | A dedicated subset of a carrier closed under specific operations. |
| **Исходный носитель** | **Source carrier** | The domain of origination prior to mapping or projection. |
| **Осуществление** | **Realization / Instantiation** | A concrete mathematical instance satisfying a given formal specification. |
| **Общее осуществление** | **Shared realization / Common instantiation** | A simultaneous realization underlying multiple distinct readings or projections. |
| **Свидетель** | **Witness** | An auxiliary element, state, or record proving the validity of a transition or equality. |
| **Свидетель грани** | **Face witness** | A witness localized to a boundary, facet, or intersection of constraints. |
| **Общий свидетель** | **Shared witness / Common witness** | A joint witness certifying the compatibility of independent partial readings. |
| **Изнанка (ответа / чтения)** | **Reverse side / Underside (of the answer / reading)** | The fibers or preimage components collapsed by a non-injective reading. |
| **Скрытая сторона** | **Hidden side / Latent side** | Differences or degrees of freedom present in the source model not visible in a current record. |
| **Опора (различения)** | **Fulcrum / Support (of distinction)** | An anchor, reference value, or invariant required to reassemble or orient a distinction. |
| **Метацентр** | **Metacenter** | The formal role of a balancing fulcrum relative to which different presentations are harmonized. |
| **Указатель** | **Pointer / Indicator** | An addressing mechanism selecting a point, element, or domain without declaring full metric/linear law. |
| **Наблюдатель** | **Observer** | The formal role of selecting a reading and obtaining an answer (not a physical or conscious agent). |
| **Роль доступа** | **Access role** | The specification of what queries or subcarriers are permitted to be probed. |
| **Интерфейс** | **Interface** | The explicit boundary across which data and operations are communicated between frames. |
| **Согласование** | **Coordination / Harmonization** | Ensuring that different representations or routes yield compatible, non-contradictory answers. |

### 2. Operational, Dynamic & Question Calculus / Операционное исчисление и исчисление вопросов

| Russian Term | English Term | Meaning / Formal Context in DOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Акт** | **Act** | An elementary execution step combining admissibility, source state, action, question, and event. |
| **Первый акт** | **First act** | The foundational transition from an initial state pair to distinction, action, and question. |
| **Действие ($T$)** | **Action ($T$)** | An admissible transformation on the carrier of states. |
| **Частичное действие** | **Partial action** | An action defined only on an admissible subset of states. |
| **Тождество** | **Identity** | The trivial action preserving all state distinctions ($T_0 = \mathrm{id}$). |
| **Обращение** | **Inversion** | An involution or inverse operator ($T^{-1}$ or $\sigma$) reversing an action. |
| **Составление** | **Composition** | Sequential application of actions ($T_2 \circ T_1$) or mappings. |
| **Порядок составления** | **Composition order** | The directional sequence in which operations are performed. |
| **История** | **History** | The ordered sequence of past acts, transitions, and intermediate states. |
| **Предъявление** | **Presentation / Exhibition** | Exposing an object through an explicit mapping into an observable domain. |
| **Правило ($h$)** | **Rule ($h$)** | The deterministic function extracting an answer from accessible data ($h: B \to \Omega$). |
| **Чтение ($m$)** | **Reading ($m$)** | The projection mapping states to accessible data ($m: S \to B$). |
| **Вопрос ($q$)** | **Question ($q$)** | The composite query $q = h \circ m$ assigning an answer to each state. |
| **Исходный домен вопроса** | **Source domain of the question** | The carrier set $S$ upon which question $q$ is evaluated. |
| **Встречный вопрос ($q \circ T$)** | **Counter-question / Reciprocal question ($q \circ T$)** | The pullback of a question along an action, interrogating the source state prior to transformation. |
| **Два переноса** | **Two transfers** | The dual propagation: forward pushforward of states/actions vs. backward pullback of questions. |
| **Исходное происхождение** | **Source provenance** | The tracking of the originating carrier, frame, and path that generated an expression. |
| **Прообраз** | **Preimage** | The set of source states mapping to a given answer or observable value ($q^{-1}(\omega)$). |
| **Двоичная таблица сравнения** | **Binary comparison table** | Truth table tabulating questions against states (e.g., $4 \times 4$ or $4 \times 8$). |
| **Аффинный вопрос ($W$)** | **Affine question ($W$)** | A question of the form $k \oplus ax \oplus dc$ over $\mathbb{F}_2^2$. |
| **Сбалансированный вопрос ($A_6$)** | **Balanced question ($A_6$)** | An affine question taking values $0$ and $1$ with equal multiplicity ($2$ and $2$). |
| **Фокусный вопрос ($q^*$)** | **Focal question ($q^*$)** | A distinguished reference question chosen on the active scene. |

### 3. Sufficiency, Memory & Closure / Достаточность, память и замыкание

| Russian Term | English Term | Meaning / Formal Context in DOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Достаточность** | **Sufficiency** | When an observable reading retains enough information to answer a target inquiry. |
| **Отрицательное начало** | **Negative principle / Negative origin** | Establishing what cannot be determined from a given reading without extra data. |
| **Спуск частичного действия** | **Descent of partial action** | Factoring an action through a projection to act legitimately on observable values. |
| **Условие спуска** | **Descent condition** | The commutation condition $m(T(s)) = \tilde{T}(m(s))$ ensuring action factors through reading $m$. |
| **Отказ спуска** | **Descent failure** | When an action transforms states with identical readings into states with distinct readings. |
| **Порядок уточнения** | **Refinement order** | The partial order where partition $\mathcal{P}_1 \le \mathcal{P}_2$ indicates finer discrimination. |
| **Цена независимых ответов** | **Cost of independent answers** | The entropy or bit budget required to resolve multiple uncoordinated questions. |
| **Память** | **Memory** | Retained auxiliary data sufficient to enable subsequent action or reassembly. |
| **Наиболее грубая устойчивая память** | **Coarsest stable memory** | The minimal partition that remains invariant under admissible actions and preserves target readings. |
| **Локальное замыкание** | **Local closure** | Completing a set of local observations into an algebraically or geometrically closed packet. |
| **Совместная сборка чтений** | **Joint assembly of readings** | Reconstructing a global state from compatible local patches. |
| **Ложная стыковка** | **False docking / Spurious join** | An apparent compatibility of local readings that fails to correspond to any global source state. |
| **Общий третий участник** | **Common third participant** | A shared relational anchor resolving ambiguity between two indeterminate elements. |
