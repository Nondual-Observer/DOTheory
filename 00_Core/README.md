# DOT Core Corpus

This folder contains the current English build of Distinction Observable
Theory — the ten-volume edition (2026-06-01).

The theory studies one question: how stable distinction on a finite
carrier is structured. The carrier is the finite Boolean cubes
\(Q_n=\mathbb F_2^{\,n}\); the relating operation is the complement
\(\kappa(x)=x+1^n\); the observer is its invariant, the center
\(c=\tfrac12(0^n+1^n)\). The corpus runs from the self-relation
\(\iota^2=\operatorname{id}\) to the observer as a structure out of which
the scene unfolds.

The direction of the theory is generative, not analytic: not "this object
has such an invariant," but "what structure is forced by the primitive."
The rigor of this course rests on **forcedness** — what is generated must
be the unique structure the primitive admits under neutrality (Volume 0,
§0.5 and §2.5). This is a principle running through the whole corpus, not a
separate document: it is introduced at the start of Volume 0 and operates
everywhere a structure "unfolds."

## Reading order

1. [Volume 0. The First Beginning](DOT_Volume_0_Pervonachalo_EN.md)
   — absolute invariant, carrier as orbit of the self-relation, complement
   \(\kappa\), observer-center, poles and active scene, lift, threshold of
   threeness.
2. [Volume 1. Rank 3: the home of threeness](DOT_Volume_1_Rang_3_EN.md)
   — active scene of rank 3, three Borromean directions, relational
   anatomy \(R_1,R_2,R_3\), octahedron, cycle operator, \(U_3/\kappa\cong PG(1,2)\).
3. [Volume 2. The break 2×2=4](DOT_Volume_2_Razryv_4_EN.md)
   — birth of the interior layer, end of Borromean holding, return of the
   rank-3 scene as core, Fano plane \(U_4/\kappa\cong PG(2,2)\); rank 5.
4. [Volume 3. Verticality](DOT_Volume_3_Vertikal_EN.md)
   — boundary and coboundary, \(\partial^2=0\), cone, tower of complexes,
   adjoint pair \(\Lambda\dashv\pi\), reductions as descent.
5. [Volume 4. The Helicoid](DOT_Volume_4_Gelikoid_EN.md)
   — intra-scene rotation, Singer cycle \(PG(n-2,2)\), addition of
   rotation and ascent into a screw, incommensurability of the step.
6. [Volume 5. The Observer as a Through-Thread](DOT_Volume_5_Nablyudatel_EN.md)
   — three guises of the observer reduced to one, germ of the lift, the
   observer as axis of the helicoid.
7. [Volume 6. External Readings as Projections](DOT_Volume_6_Chteniya_EN.md)
   — reading as projection, atlas (logical, color, spectral, semantic,
   arithmetic), transition maps.
8. [Volume 7. The Structure of the Observer](DOT_Volume_7_Struktura_Nablyudatelya_EN.md)
   — observer as \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\)-structure with rotation, complement
   as Weyl involution, scene as unfolding of the structure, operator tower.
9. [Volume 8. Arithmetic Realization](DOT_Volume_8_Arifmeticheskaya_Realizaciya_EN.md)
   — divisor lattice as carrier, \(D(p_1\cdots p_n)\cong Q_n\), the number
   30 as the rank-3 scene, the second axis of growth (multiplicity).
10. [Volume 9. Arithmetic Structure Behind the Cube](DOT_Volume_9_Arifmeticheskaya_Struktura_EN.md)
    — multiplicative monoid, recovery discipline, residue bridge,
    horizontal \(\mathfrak{sl}_3\).

## Status

This is the core corpus — the new principal presentation of the theory.
Volumes 0–4 and 7 form the rigorous backbone; Volumes 6, 8, 9 gather
readings and realizations with explicit status tags (realization /
bridge / horizon). The historical DOT documents and bridge notes lie in
the folders `02_Bridges`, `03_Appendix`, `04_Articles` and serve for
cross-checking, bridges, and archive.
